Privacy policy

Fadak Sudoku · 파닥 스도쿠
Effective and last updated: September 29, 2026

This policy covers the Fadak Sudoku app and this information website. The Fadak Sudoku developer operates the app and handles privacy requests at devfadakworld@gmail.com. The app does not require a separate user account. The Android production package is com.fadak.sudoku; the test package is com.fadak.sudoku.dev.

Current distribution

The basic test APK currently provided is an offline version without internet permission or advertising and billing SDKs. The advertising and purchase-verification sections below apply only to versions that include those features. A public release using the purchase server will not be enabled until its operator and hosting country are settled and this policy is updated.

1. Game data on your device

Puzzle progress, entries and notes, marks, undo history, bookmarks, completion records, daily challenges, learning progress, language and appearance settings are stored on your device. They support resuming puzzles and personal records and are not transmitted to a developer server. We do not include a separate behavioral analytics SDK.

The app does not request contacts, camera, microphone or precise location permissions. Local game data is removed when you clear the app's storage or uninstall it. Deleting completion records alone does not delete your other puzzle progress.

2. Backups and external links you choose

When exporting or importing a backup, the app reads or writes only the file you choose through the Android document picker. Backups include game progress and settings, but not purchase tokens or purchase entitlements. Backups are unencrypted JSON files: choose where to store or share them carefully. The developer does not automatically upload them.

If you choose a cloud drive, that provider's policy applies. Uninstalling the app does not delete an exported backup; delete those files separately. Opening a learning reference or policy link uses an external browser, and the destination site's policy applies.

3. Versions with advertising

Versions with advertising use Google AdMob and Google's consent tools. Applicable consent and choice flows are provided; supported choices can be revisited in the app's privacy settings. Ads are not requested while a purchase or restoration is awaiting verification or a valid ad-permission policy cannot be confirmed.

The Google advertising SDK may process IP addresses and approximate location inferred from them, ad and app interactions, performance and diagnostic information, and identifiers such as advertising IDs and device/account identifiers for ad delivery, measurement and fraud prevention. Non-personalized ads may still involve data needed for delivery and security. Google's policies and applicable consent choices govern its processing and retention. See Google's Privacy Policy and the advertising SDK data disclosure.

4. Google Play purchases and verification

In versions with billing, Google Play processes payments and the payment account. The developer does not receive full card numbers or full payment-method details. To validate, restore and reflect refunds of the remove-ads entitlement, a minimal verification service processes the purchase token, application package ID, product ID, verification status, acknowledgment status and verification time.

The service encrypts purchase tokens at rest and uses their SHA-256 hashes as support purchase references. Its ledger does not store puzzle boards or notes, Google account emails, advertising IDs, card numbers or order IDs. Tokens are verified through the Google Play Developer API. Valid purchase records are kept while needed to maintain and restore the entitlement. Confirmed revoked/refunded records are configured for deletion 90 days after their final check. You can request deletion by email using your purchase reference.

Request IP addresses are temporarily used to limit excessive requests; the application server does not put request bodies, purchase tokens or IP addresses in ordinary logs. The hosting provider and country, proxy logging and retention configuration will be added before the service is publicly deployed. A network failure alone does not revoke a previously verified remove-ads entitlement.

5. Support messages

If you contact us by email, we use your email address, message and any screenshots or diagnostic details you choose to attach to investigate and reply. Copying diagnostic information does not send it: you can review it before sharing. Do not send passwords, card numbers or full purchase tokens. A backup you attach may contain game records.

Support email is handled through Gmail. Resolved messages are ordinarily deleted within 90 days of the last reply. We may keep information longer where needed for an ongoing dispute or a legal retention duty, and explain the reason and period on request.

6. This website and international processing

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages. The developer adds no advertising or visitor analytics scripts or tracking cookies. GitHub may process technical information, including visitors' IP addresses, to provide and secure its service. See the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Google and GitHub services may process information outside your country. Their published policies describe their service providers, locations, safeguards and retention. Specific international-transfer information for the purchase server will be published after its hosting location is determined and before it is made public.

7. Your choices and requests

There is no separate app account to close. Clear local data through Android's app storage settings. This cannot be undone; export a backup first if needed. Delete exported backups separately.

You can email us to request applicable access, correction, deletion or restriction of support and purchase-verification information held by the developer. We verify requests using the minimum information necessary and handle them under applicable law. If a retention duty or purchase dispute prevents immediate deletion, we explain why. After verification records are deleted, restoring a purchase may require verification through Google Play again. A data-deletion request is not itself a refund request.

8. Children and policy changes

The app is a general-audience puzzle game and does not encourage children to provide personal information. A parent or guardian who believes a child's information was sent to us can contact us. Age and regional settings for versions with advertising are reviewed before public release.

When data, purposes, retention or providers change, we update this page and its date and provide an in-app notice or consent flow where required. Material changes are announced before they take effect.
